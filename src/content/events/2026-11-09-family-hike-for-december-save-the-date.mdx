  ***Family Nature Hike Series – Hike #9!***  **Herrontown Woods**


  [RSVP here!](https://www.punchbowl.com/parties/4e626bf0c414ba2d7622)


  Note the hike start time of 11 AM - please come early (from 9 to 11) if you'd like to hang out over some coffee &amp; tea.


  Join us for the **NINTH hike in a family-friendly nature series** designed to help children and parents connect with the outdoors while learning how to care for the ecosystems around us. Bring your family for a relaxed and engaging hike where kids can explore the woods, discover plants and wildlife, and learn how healthy ecosystems work.  Our goal is simple: **help kids fall in love with nature so they grow up wanting to protect it.**


  **COME EARLY to** socialize in nature 9-11 AM: a pop-up “May’s Cafe” in the Botanical Art Garden (located next the the parking lot) with coffee and baked treats available for purchase.


  **Who Should Come**


  - Families with children ages 3-14

  - Parents who want their kids to **get outside, learn, and explore**

  - Anyone interested in **nature, conservation, and community**


  All children must be **accompanied by an adult at all times**.


  **About the Series**


  - **Monthly family hikes** (typically Sundays at 9:30 AM) alternating between Princeton and Hopewell trails.

  - Hosted by HVRSD ecoPTO &amp; Littlebrook PTO

  - Organized by Kari Lloyd, K-5 STEAM Teacher at Littlebrook and member of HVRSD (Hopewell Schools) ecoPTO

  - Future hikes will explore other local trails with plenty of parking and space for families.


  **A Few Guidelines**


  - **All children must be accompanied by an adult at all times**.

  - Please **stay on the trails**

  - Dress for the weather and wear comfortable hiking shoes

  - Bring water and curiosity!

  - Remember to check your family for ticks!  

  - Consider wearing long pants to reduce the risk of contact with poison ivy,.


  We’re excited to build a community of families who **explore, learn, and care for nature together**. Hope to see you on the trail! 


  Don't wait, [RSVP now](https://www.punchbowl.com/parties/4e626bf0c414ba2d7622) as spots are limited.
