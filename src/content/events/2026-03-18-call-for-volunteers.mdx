description: >-
  In 2025, the HVRSD was awarded a Climate Literacy for Community Resilience
  Grant from the Department of Education to develop new high-quality,
  standards-based climate change education curricula for K-12 students and to
  encourage student-centered experiential learning opportunities and engagement
  in location-based climate change solutions. The Watershed Institute is a
  community partner for this grant that is assisting with installing native
  plant gardens at all six district schools. These gardens will serve as living
  classrooms for students to apply the knowledge they learned in the classroom.


  These are big projects and need all the help we can find! If you are interested in assisting, please look at the sign-up genius to see how you can help at each schools to get the garden areas prepped for students to plant. The general tasks for each day will be sod cutting, making plug plant holes (maybe), and creating the inlet/outlet structures for the gardens.


  If you are not able to make it for the full three hours, please just note this on the signup genius form.
