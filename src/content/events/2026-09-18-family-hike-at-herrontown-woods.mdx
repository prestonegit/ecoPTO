  Bring the whole family for a relaxed, engaging walk in the woods where kids can explore, discover native plants and wildlife, and learn how healthy forests work. Our goal is simple: help kids fall in love with nature so they grow up wanting to protect it. This series is organized by ecoPTO's Kari Lloyd, and designed to help children and parents connect with nature while learning how to care for the ecosystems around us.


  **Who Should Come**


  - \*Families with children ages 3–14

  - \*Parents who want their kids to get outside, learn, and explore

  - \*Anyone interested in nature, conservation, and community

  - *All children must be accompanied by an adult.*


  **Meet-up at 9:30** for a pop-up “May’s Cafe” in the Botanical Art Garden (located next the the parking lot) for coffee and baked treats available for purchase.  


  **Hikes depart at 10am:**


  - **Younger Children Group:** Meandering short hike, led by outdoor education leader Nicole Langdo, founder of Painted Oak Nature School, who specializes in hands-on nature exploration for younger kids.  

  - 

  - **Older Children Groups:** Join Friends of Herrontown Woods leaders for your choice of either 1 mile or 2 miles.  Leaders will guide families in observing the forest ecosystem and learning about conservation.


  [**RSVP NOW**](https://donorbox.org/events/962594/steps/choose_tickets?utm_source=qrcode)!  We are limiting this event to 25 families!  Tickets are $20 per family and will sell out.


  *All proceeds from this event will go to Friends of Herrontown Woods, the non-profit that maintains and builds the magic and ecosystem health of Herrontown Woods.*


  \*Contact Kari Lloyd ([karinlloyd@princetonk12.org](mailto:karinlloyd@princetonk12.org)) for a discount code for financial assistance if needed.
