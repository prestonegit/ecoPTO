  \*Join us for the FIFTH hike in a new family-friendly nature series designed to help children and parents connect with the outdoors while learning how to care for the ecosystems around us. Bring your family for a relaxed and engaging hike where kids can explore the marshlands, surrounding woodland, and nature center while discovering plants and wildlife.  Let's collectively learn how healthy ecosystems work. Our goal is simple: help kids fall in love with nature so they grow up wanting to protect it.


  Come for 9:30am coffee, tea, and baked goods. All proceeds from this little pre-hike "hang" will go to support the HVRSD ecoPTO and Mercer County Parks.  


  **How THIS Hike Will Work**


  Add a unique element of fun to your family nature walk! Aqua Marooned is a trail game that will have you exploring the outdoors while engaging in simple challenges of observation and creativity. Meet at the Tulpehaking Nature Center to start your trail adventure.


  **Who Should Come**


  * Families with children ages 3-14

  * Parents who want their kids to get outside, learn, and explore

  * Anyone interested in nature, conservation, and community


  **About the Series**


  * Monthly family hikes (typically Sundays with 9:30am meet-ip and 10am start) typically alternating between Princeton and Hopewell trails, but sometimes new educational spots, like this one.

  * The overall series is hosted by HVRSD ecoPTO & Littlebrook PTO

  * Organized by Kari Lloyd, K-5 STEAM Teacher at Littlebrook and member of HVRSD (Hopewell Schools) ecoPTO

  * Future hikes will explore other local trails with plenty of parking and space for families.


  **A Few Guidelines**


  * Please stay on the trails

  * All children must be accompanied by an adult at all times.

  * Bring water and curiosity! 

  * Dress for the weather and wear comfortable hiking shoes; Long pants are recommended. 

  * Be sure to check for ticks after your hike!
