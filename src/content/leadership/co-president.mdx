name: Gabe Dominguez
title: Co-President
bio: "Every morning, Gabe bikes his two kids to Hopewell Elementary with a
  speaker bumping, because sustainability should have a good soundtrack. When he
  heard the [district’s vision](https://www.hvrsd.org/page/sustainability) for
  an “environmentally just and sustainable future” harmonizing with his own
  private wish for \"an ecotopian school day\" he knew it was a choral piece,
  not a solo; so he co-founded ecoPTO to make it easier for folks to add their
  harmony. Before moving to NJ in 2022, Gabe co-founded [San Francisco Bicycle
  Music
  Festival](https://www.studioecotopia.com/cultural-constructions/san-francisco\
  -bicycle-music-festival), led the band [SHAKE YOUR
  PEACE!](http://www.shakeyourpeace.com/), and made [street
  theater](https://www.studioecotopia.com/cultural-constructions/ecotopia-now!)
  with school climate strikers. These days you'll find him DJ'ing school events,
  singing around town with his duo [Tiny Home](https://www.tinyhomemusic.com/),
  or whistling while he works: building and repairing wooden structures using
  non-toxic carpentry and natural building techniques ([Studio
  Ecotopia](https://www.studioecotopia.com/))."
