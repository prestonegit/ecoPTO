history_title: Our History
history_content: >-
  The spark for ecoPTO ignited in the spring of 2023 when the Hopewell Valley
  Regional School District invited our community to participate in a Strategic
  Planning series. A small group of dedicated parents quickly discovered a
  shared passion for ideas like eco-literacy, collective resilience, and outdoor
  learning, using this common language to gain momentum throughout the meetings.


  Our collaborative efforts proved successful: these core values were not only integrated into the District's five Strategic Goals for the next three to five years, but they also brought together a team of HVRSD "rockstars" who helped shape our mission and vision.


  ecoPTO's mission is to gather and mobilize the HVRSD educational community to actualize the District’s aspirations for Youth and Earth Wellbeing (YEW). It’s a simple mission with a powerful acronym—we care about YEW. After a year of very dedicated work (and some bumps and bruises along the way), we were granted formal PTO status by the Board of Education in August 2025.


  We firmly believe that every member of our District has unique talents and expertise to contribute. This collective action is the key to building significant community resilience and connection that extends far beyond traditional notions of wellbeing. With an intention to uplift and collaborate with our current PTOs, teachers, staff, and administrators, we foresee a future so bright, you’ll have to wear shades!
