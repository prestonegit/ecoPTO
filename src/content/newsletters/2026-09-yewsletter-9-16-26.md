  Hey YEW!


  "How are you?" is often treated as a polite reflex—a routine greeting or an easy way to fill a silence. At ecoPTO, we ask this question with genuine intention: *How are our Youth? How is our Earth? How is our community’s Wellbeing?*


  At the center of our YEWniverse (what a pun!) is a simple, shared purpose: energizing our educational community to (re)build a connection with the natural world. While the mission is easy to champion, the daily reality our families and schools face tells a different story. In his 2005 book *Last Child in the Woods*, Richard Louv coined the term "Nature-Deficit Disorder." While not a medical diagnosis, it offers vital language for an undeniable cultural reality. Our days are increasingly lived indoors, defined by screen time, overflowing to-do lists, and structured obligations. This is neither a personal shortcoming nor an institutional critique; it is simply the pattern of modern life.


  This month, we invite you to join us and  intentionally shift this pattern, together. Because when we integrate Nature back into the rhythm of daily life (both in the classroom and at home!) the benefits ripple:


  - **Curiosity and learning expand**, as outdoor exploration naturally sparks engagement, problem-solving, and creativity.

  - **Mental and physical health improve**, offering families, students, and educators a meaningful antidote to daily stress.

  - **Community bonds strengthen**, rooted in shared responsibility of the environment we call home.


  There is so much thoughtful, dedicated work happening across our district and in our households. ecoPTO is grateful to partner with families as well as  educational leaders as we explore small, intentional ways to step outside of our own boxes, and (re)connect with what sustains us all.\



  Writing on behalf of bugs, books, and brain health,


  Kristin, ecoPTO Co-President\

  Karen, ecoPTO communications
