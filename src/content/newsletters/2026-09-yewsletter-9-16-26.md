  Hey YEW,


  The question of 'how are you' comes up a lot. It's a pleasantry, a way to say hello, an introduction, and many times, an awkward silence-filler. At ecoPTO, we genuinely want to know though how are you? How are our Youth? How is our Earth? And how is our community's wellbeing? 


  Although ecoPTO has energy for a whole lot of projects and ideas, the nucleus of YEW is about building and prioritizing a love of Nature. This mission is easy to get behind. But let's flip the coin over and take a micro-view: **Nature Deficit Disorder**. Coined in 2005 by Richard Louv, author of *Last Child in the Woods*, Nature Deficit Disorder is a non-medical term that puts language to a void in our modern lives. We are all nature-deficient, but let me be clear, this is not a personal fault or failure, and this is not a judgment bur rather a fact of the current American culture. Humans spend their days indoors and engaged in structured activities- work meetings, traditional education, the forward. facing role of technology, afterschool obligations- to name a few.


  What if our collective mindset began to intentionally change together, turning *away* from cultural norms, and turning *towards* Nature? How would our family life function differently? How could our mental and physical health improve? What might our educational community gain? How can student learning expand, and teacher wellbeing improve? There's so much we are all doing, and ecoPTO is grateful to be a part of it. And yet, there is so much more to do in shifting American culture to be more closely aligned with the very ways human life is sustained.\

  \

  The winds of change are blowing wild and free,\

  Kristin, writing on behalf of ecoPTO
