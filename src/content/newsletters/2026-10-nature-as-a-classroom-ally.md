  *Nature as a Classroom Ally* means recognizing that the natural world is a
  built-in partner that calms young nervous systems, sparks effortless
  curiosity, and brings everyday lessons to life for both teachers and our
  children.


  This coming January 2027, ecoPTO aims to gift a curated **Nature Connection Gift Box** to every Kindergarten and 1st-grade classroom across all four HVRSD elementary schools. Each collection is filled with nature-based books and hands-on resources grounded in **gratitude, reciprocity, exploration, and wonder**. Together, they give teachers and young learners an easy, inviting way to weave nature into every school day, both indoors and out!


  Our goal is to bring this project to life at **zero cost to school or district budgets**. To help us reach our goal, we are thrilled to partner with local photographer and Bear Tavern parent **Brianna Gosselin**, who is offering a special thank-you mini photo session for donors who contribute $75 or more.


  **Mini Photo Session Details:**


  - **When:** Saturday, November 14 &amp; Sunday, November 15

  - **Where:** Baldpate Mountain (relaxed, open meadow setting)

  - **What:** A candid, playful 20-minute session including at least 5 edited digital images (no stiff posing required!)


  **How You Can Support:**


  **Step 1:** Make your donation [here](https://ecopto.org/donate/) *(every contribution helps, and $75 sponsors a full classroom box).* 


  **Step 2:** Contributing $75 or more? Book your thank you photo session [here](https://briannagosselin.pixieset.com/booking/ecopto-kindergarten-book-boxes). Spots are limited, don't delay!


  100% of funds raised will go directly towards our gift box initiative. Thank you for your generosity and ongoing dedication to connecting our students and educators to the natural world!
