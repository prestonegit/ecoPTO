## Design Context

### Users
Parents, teachers, and community members involved with or interested in the Hopewell Valley Regional School District (HVRSD) ecoPTO. They are looking to participate in sustainability initiatives and need to feel heard regarding environmental practices in the district, even when systemic changes are slow.

### Brand Personality
Sustainable, community-focused, progressive.

### Aesthetic Direction
The current aesthetic (earthy Terracotta Brown and Soft Peach with elegant Playfair Display and modern Plus Jakarta Sans typography) is working well. The design should feel fresh and innovative, visually communicating that the ecoPTO is "doing something new." The focus is on optimizing and polishing the existing foundation to make it feel more cohesive and impactful, rather than starting from scratch.

### Design Principles
1. **Amplify Voices**: Design elements should make users feel their contributions and concerns are heard and valued. Interfaces should be inviting and communicative.
2. **Community First**: Prioritize clarity, accessibility, and welcoming layouts that encourage participation over rigid, institutional structures.
3. **Showcase Innovation**: Use modern, polished UI optimizations (purposeful motion, clear hierarchy, refined spacing) to visually reinforce that this is a progressive, forward-thinking initiative.
4. **Refine and Elevate**: Build upon the existing warm palette and typography, focusing on exceptional execution, consistency, and subtle enhancements (like better rhythm and contrast) rather than changing the core aesthetic.
